# Underneath

AI app supporting families on ADHD, autism and CAMHS waiting lists.

## What it does

Underneath supports families from the moment they join a waiting list for ADHD, autism, CAMHS or SEND support. Parents log daily check-ins in 30 seconds. Gemini AI analyses each entry and returns a personalised insight, possible triggers and an exact script of what to say to their child in that moment.

## Live product

- 13 families registered
- 29 check-ins logged
- Weekly active users: 12
- Live at www.underneathfamily.com

## Revenue model

Underneath is currently in free pilot phase with 13 families. Revenue model is B2B licensing to local authorities and NHS trusts. No revenue yet — launching commercially following pilot completion August 2026.
